Clinical Guidelines for Type 2 Diabetes Management (2024 Edition)
Section 1: Diagnosis Criteria
Type 2 Diabetes is diagnosed when:
• Fasting Plasma Glucose ≥ 126 mg/dL
• HbA1c ≥ 6.5%
• Random Plasma Glucose ≥ 200 mg/dL with symptoms

Section 3: Treatment Protocol
First-line treatment for Type 2 Diabetes:
• Metformin
• Lifestyle modification
• Regular glucose monitoring
Patients without diabetes should have:
• HbA1c below 5.7%
• Fasting glucose below 100 mg/dL
Section 4: Risk Notes
Hypertension is defined as blood pressure ≥ 140/90 mmHg.
Metformin should not be prescribed to patients with severe renal impairment.
